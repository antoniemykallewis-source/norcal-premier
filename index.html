04 / A LITTLE HELP GOES A LONG WAY

STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Find the right person ↗
I don’t know our competition. Where do I start?

Ask your team manager for the competition name and age group. You can also find your club in the NorCal club directory or follow your team in the NorCal app.

Did our game time or field change?

Check the current team schedule in the NorCal app and confirm with your team manager. The season calendar shows competition dates; it is not a substitute for your team’s exact kickoff and field assignment.

Where do I find parking, field numbers and venue rules?

State Cup families can use the official Field Maps & Guidelines. For league games, use your team’s assigned venue and confirm site-specific details with your club.

Do parents handle team check-in?

Coaches and managers handle the game day roster and digital check-in. Parents should follow their team’s arrival instructions. The manager guide explains the roster, player cards and referee QR code.

Is help available in Spanish?

Sí. NorCal provides instrucciones de la app en español and a Spanish handbook linked from the handbook page.

The app isn’t working. Who can help?

Contact appsupport@norcalpremier.com. For competition questions, use the NorCal staff directory.
